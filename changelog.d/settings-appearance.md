### Added

- Settings ▸ General ▸ Appearance: System, Light or Dark for the whole app, chosen from three pictures.
- Settings ▸ General ▸ Appearance ▸ Note Pages: Light, Dark or Match App. Dark pages show the paper
  dark and ink, text and equations with their lightness inverted, keeping their colors; pictures,
  PDFs and videos are not inverted. Notes are stored and exported unchanged.
- Mac: Settings ▸ General ▸ Drag to Finder chooses what a note dragged out of the note list
  becomes: PDF (the default), Markdown or HTML.

### Changed

- Settings is a list of groups (General, Writing, Audio & Transcription, Sync & Devices,
  Backups & History, About), each on its own page; on a Mac the groups are in the Settings
  window's sidebar.
