### Added

- Sync: Vault Details (Sync Targets ▸ Details and Repair, and Settings ▸ Storage) shows the space the vault takes on this
  device and, per sync target, its last sync, the devices it holds and the files a sync refused, which Repair can adopt,
  export or delete. Sync Targets can add a WebDAV server to a vault. The welcome screen lists every vault in one list
  with its sync status. CLI: `sempere store info`, `sempere store quarantine`, `store target add --check`.
