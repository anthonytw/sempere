### Added

- Note Insights in the app (Settings ▸ Note Insights; off until you turn it on): a short summary, keywords and related
  notes for each note, made on your devices only with Apple's on-device model and stored encrypted in the vault. Open
  them from a note's More menu or the note list's context menu; search also finds notes by their summaries and keywords;
  related notes are drawn on a map. One work device does the processing
  (any device that can run the models; an iPhone or iPad while charging) and names quick voice notes from their
  transcripts in a format you choose. Turning it off deletes the insights on every device.
