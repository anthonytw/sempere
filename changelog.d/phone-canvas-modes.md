### Changed

- iPhone: the note view has three separate modes, shown by the two top-bar buttons: Reading (both off: one
  finger scrolls, nothing draws or selects), Hand (one finger scrolls, a tap selects an item, a hold shows its
  menu, a drag moves it; no palette) and Annotate (a finger draws with the palette, two fingers scroll). A tap on
  the lit button goes back to Reading, a tap on the other switches straight to its mode; the palette's Hand
  switches to Hand mode. The Hand no longer turns Annotate on.

### Fixed

- iPhone: a pageless note no longer goes back to needing two fingers to scroll a moment after Annotate is
  turned off.
- iPhone: turning the pencil on or off with the Hand on can no longer leave both buttons off while a finger
  still draws.
