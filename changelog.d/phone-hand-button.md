### Added

- iPhone: a Hand button beside Annotate in the note's top bar. One tap turns on selection mode (one finger scrolls, a tap
  selects an item, a hold shows its menu); tap it again to go back to drawing.

### Changed

- iPhone: the note's overflow menu no longer lists Recordings twice, and the Pages menu now sits right after Insert,
  where it is visible without scrolling. The iPad's "…" menu loses the same duplicate "Recordings…" entry
  (the Recordings menu's "All Recordings…" and Note > Recordings… on the Mac remain).
