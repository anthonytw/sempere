### Added

- Make a Copy…: File ▸ Make a Copy… (and a button in the banner of a read-only vault) copies a vault's notes, as they
  are now, into a new vault of your own with its own recovery key, attachments and recordings included. It works for a
  vault opened with its recovery key, one from a newer version, or one whose device list this device could not confirm,
  and the copy can be changed. `sempere store copy` does the same on the command line.
