### Changed

- iPad and Mac: notes open in Reading, with the iPhone's three modes on every device: Reading (scroll, nothing
  draws or selects), Hand (select, move and resize items) and Annotate (the palette, drawing), shown by the same
  two top-bar buttons. On an iPad a Pencil touch in Reading or Hand switches to Annotate and draws that stroke.
  The Mac's Tools menu has Annotate (⇧⌘P) and Hand (⌥⌘7); a tool picked there switches to Annotate.
- The Show/Hide Tool Palette button is now the Annotate button (press and hold for the compact palette), and the
  Select toggle is the Hand.
- The note's toolbar has one order and one set of items on the iPhone, the iPad and the Mac; the iPhone moves
  what does not fit its top bar into the overflow menu, in the same order. Page actions and the layout switch
  are in a Pages menu on every device; Text and the object eraser's size show while annotating.
- iPhone: notebooks with sub-notebooks fold and unfold again, with a chevron at the end of the row; a tap on
  the row still opens the notebook.

### Removed

- The Hand in the tool palette (the top bar's Hand is the only one).

### Fixed

- iPad: a note's recordings list could be reached only by holding the Recordings button; All Recordings… is
  in the overflow menu on every device.
