### Added

- Note Insights: a map of related notes (All Notes' menu, or Settings ▸ Note Insights ▸ Map of Related Notes). Notes
  sit in labeled groups that split into smaller ones as you zoom in, lines join related notes, and a tap opens a note;
  notes this device placed itself are dashed, and a device that has to build its own map says so. A list form shows
  the same groups for VoiceOver and small screens.
- Note Insights: topic summaries. "Summary" on a notebook or tag with at least 3 summarized notes shows a generated
  paragraph and the notes to check it against; it is marked when those notes changed. The work device makes them in
  the background, at most once a day each, and Regenerate makes one now. `sempere insights topic --list` lists them.
