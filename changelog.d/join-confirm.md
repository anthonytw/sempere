### Changed

- Joining a device: both screens now show the six words and you tap They Match after comparing all six, instead of
  typing them. Typing the words and scanning the code stay under "Type the words instead". Add Device warns when two
  or more devices are asking to join, and They Match waits 1.5 seconds after the words show. Tapping is as safe as
  typing only if you compare all six words.
