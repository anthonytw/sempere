### Fixed

- iPad and Mac: a pageless note (such as one imported from Notability) zooms out below the fitted
  width, to half of it, centered, and still snaps back to the fitted width.
- iPad: the item menu from a held finger goes as soon as the page scrolls, zooms or is written on,
  or another touch lands; a hold that turns into a scroll no longer brings it up afterwards.
- iPad: a zoom no longer jumps back to the fitted width under the fingers in the middle of a pinch.
- Scrolling and zooming a long note: the paper's ruling is drawn only near the screen, not for the
  whole note on every frame.
